OUR STORY

Built on Craft. Driven by Passion.

Heppyz Salon was born in Bhagalpur with one mission — to give this city a salon experience that matches the standards of any metro. Not just a haircut. An experience.

OUR JOURNEY

The Timeline

2024

Founded in Bhagalpur with a vision for premium unisex salon services. The atelier opens its doors with a commitment to precision and excellence.

2024

Launched with full-service hair, skin, and makeup offerings. Every stylist trained in modern techniques and international standards.

2024

First 100 clients served within the opening month. Word spreads — Bhagalpur has its premium destination.

2025

Expanded service menu with bridal packages, advanced skin treatments, and editorial makeup. The atelier evolves.
